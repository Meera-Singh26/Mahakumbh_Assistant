Emergency & Safety Guides

Stampede Prevention & Safety

1. Always move with the flow of the crowd. Do not walk against it. 2. Keep your hands folded in front of your chest like a boxer to create breathing space. 3. If you drop something, do not bend down to pick it up in a dense crowd. 4. If you fall, curl into a ball, cover your head with your hands, and protect your chest.

Heat & Exhaustion First Aid

1. Drink water and ORS packets regularly, even if you are not thirsty. 2. Find shade immediately if you experience dizziness, headache, or excessive sweating. 3. Loosen tight clothing and apply cool water to the skin. 4. Contact the nearest medical booth or dial 108 if symptoms worsen.
